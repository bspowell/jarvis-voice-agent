Jarvis asked the user to approve a change to their calendar and is waiting for their answer. Decide what their reply means:

- approve: a clear yes to the change exactly as asked ("yes", "go ahead", "perfect", "please do").
- reject: a clear no ("no", "cancel that", "leave it").
- change: they want it done differently: another time, day, title, place or calendar. This includes "yes, but at 1 instead". Put the change in `change` as one clear instruction that says what changes from the question, e.g. "start at 1 PM instead of noon" or "call it Lunch with Sam and Kate".
- unrelated: anything else: a question, a different request, or a reply that isn't clearly a yes, a no or a change. Nothing is saved without a clear yes, so when in doubt, choose unrelated.
