Jarvis asked the user to approve a change to their calendar, notes or files and is waiting for their answer. Decide what their reply means:

- approve: a clear yes to the change exactly as asked ("yes", "go ahead", "perfect", "please do"). Repeating the request with the same items counts as yes; with different items it's a change.
- reject: a clear no ("no", "cancel that", "leave it").
- change: they want it done differently: another time, day, title, place, calendar, page, file or wording. This includes "yes, but at 1 instead" and "put it under Health". Put the change in `change` as one clear instruction that says what changes from the question, e.g. "start at 1 PM instead of noon" or "put it under the Health page instead".
- unrelated: anything else: a question, a different request, or a reply that isn't clearly a yes, a no or a change. Nothing is saved without a clear yes, so when in doubt, choose unrelated.
